# Rajasthan Royals — Pre-Auction Player Evaluation

Seven independent evaluation axes, no blended score.

- **[Batting](/batting)** — genuine combinable filters (season, team, phase,
  opponent bowler type, home/away), plus batting role and temperament.
- **[Bowling](/bowling)** — same filtering approach (season, team, phase,
  opponent batting hand, home/away), plus engagement band and trust marks.
- **[Fielding](/fielding)** — percentile rank within WK / non-WK peer groups.
- **[SAV](/sav)** — Gaussian-kernel situation-aware value, venue-normalized.
- **[MVP Points](/mvp)** — reconstructed points formula, batting and bowling
  reported separately.
- **[Teams](/teams)** — full roster by team and season, with career-level
  flags (Captain/Keeper/Overseas), Role Scarcity category membership and
  scores, and Aura status all shown together here rather than on separate
  pages.

Everything on this site is generated directly from SQL views in the project's
Neon database (`floral-recipe-26705265`) — no metric here is computed outside
the database.
